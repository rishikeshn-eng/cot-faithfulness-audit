Chain-of-thought monitorability

Do reasoning models say when they use a hint?

A reproducible hint-injection benchmark for chain-of-thought faithfulness: 4,000 questions (MMLU, ARC-Challenge, GSM8K) × 5 hint types, run on Gemini thinking models (with thought summaries) and a local Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct. When a model switches its answer to the hinted option, how often does its visible reasoning admit the hint was used?

In plain words

AI models can “show their working”. We slipped them a hint and checked: they used it, then usually wrote out reasoning that never mentions it.

Uptake and verbalisation by hint type

Uptake = share of questions where the model's un-hinted answer differed from the hint and the hinted answer matched it. Verbalised = share of uptake cases whose visible reasoning (thought summary or final response) acknowledges using the hint (LLM judge, checked against a 100-case manual audit). "…in final response only" ignores the thought summary, which end users of the API normally do not see. Error bars are 95% bootstrap CIs over questions.

Can a CoT monitor catch hint use?
